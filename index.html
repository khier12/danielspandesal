The Story Behind It

A Filipino Classic, Reimagined

Pandesal — the soft, cloud-like bread roll dusted in breadcrumbs — has graced Filipino breakfast tables for generations. But this version does something beautiful: it weaves in malunggay, the miracle moringa leaf, adding a gentle earthiness and a gorgeous green colour to every bite.

The most nourishing things come from the simplest ingredients.

Packed with vitamins A, C, and iron, malunggay transforms everyday pandesal into a wholesome superfood bread you can feel good about feeding your family.

The Recipe

Ingredients & Method

Simple pantry staples, one miracle leaf, one incredible bread

🧺

Ingredients

Makes 12 pieces

🌿Malunggay (Moringa) leaves1 cup
🌾All-purpose flour3 cups
🧪Instant yeast2¼ tsp
🍬White sugar¼ cup
🧂Fine salt1 tsp
🧈Unsalted butter, softened3 tbsp
🥚Large egg1 pc
🥛Warm milk (40°C)¾ cup
🍞Fine breadcrumbs½ cup
👨‍🍳

Method

Step-by-step instructions

1
🌿 Prep Malunggay

Wash leaves, strip from stems. Blanch 30 seconds in hot water, drain and pat dry.

⏱ 5 min
2
🥣 Make the Dough

Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt. Add butter, egg, warm milk, and malunggay. Knead 8–10 min until smooth.

⏱ 15 min
3
⏳ First Rise

Cover with a damp cloth. Rest in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled.

⏱ 60 min
4
🍞 Shape & Coat

Punch down, divide into 12 balls. Roll generously in breadcrumbs. Arrange on a tray.

⏱ 10 min
5
🔥 Bake

Preheat oven to 180°C. Bake 20–25 min until deeply golden. Cool 5 min. Serve warm!

⏱ 25 min
👨‍🍳
Chef's Note

For an extra soft crumb, substitute ¼ cup of the milk with fresh coconut milk. The fat content adds richness and a subtle sweetness that pairs beautifully with the earthy malunggay. Best eaten within 2 hours of baking — though in most Filipino homes, they're gone in minutes. ☕ Always serve with hot barako coffee.
